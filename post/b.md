# On Burritos

The burrito is, at its core, a engineering achievement. A self-contained meal system
with a structural tortilla shell, layered internal components, and a form factor
optimized for single-handed consumption.

Consider the wrap integrity problem. Too tight and the tortilla tears. Too loose
and the contents shift, destabilizing the load distribution. The ideal burrito
holds its shape under moderate grip pressure while remaining yielding at the bite interface.

I have been thinking about this for a long time.
