Sleep is Fake

Nobody actually sleeps 8 hours. The 8-hour recommendation assumes a contiguous block, a cool dark room, no phone, no obligations, and a brain that simply powers down on command. This describes no person I have ever met.

What we actually do is lie in the dark and think about things we said in 2011 until eventually we black out for 5 hours and call it rest.

I am very well rested.
